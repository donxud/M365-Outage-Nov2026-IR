# User advisory template: provider outage

Send through a channel that still works (SMS, intranet on separate hosting, managers). Replace the [brackets].

---

**Subject: [Service] outage: what to do, and a phishing warning**

Microsoft is currently experiencing an outage affecting [Outlook / Teams / SharePoint]. This is a problem on Microsoft's side, not your device or account. Microsoft is working on it, and we are tracking it as incident [ID].

**What to expect**
- Email may be delayed. Messages are queued, not lost, and should arrive once service recovers.
- For urgent matters, use [phone bridge / alternate channel / contact number].

**Watch out for scams**
Criminals send fake "outage" emails during incidents like this, sometimes quoting the real incident number.
- IT will never ask you to re-enter your password, approve an unexpected MFA prompt, install software, or call a number from an email to "restore access".
- Do not click links in emails about this outage. Report them to [report-phishing address/button].

We will update you by [time]. Next update channel: [channel].

[IT / Security team name]
